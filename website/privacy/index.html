Privacy policy

Effective 2026 · applies to the nimna app and nimna.app

This policy is short because there is very little to govern. Nimna has no accounts, no message storage, and no analytics. Most privacy policies describe what a company does with your data. Ours mostly describes data that does not exist.

What we collect

CategoryCollected
Name, email, phone number, or any account identifierNone — accounts do not exist
Message contentNone — messages exist only on your two phones, end-to-end encrypted in transit
Message history or backups on our sideNone — the relay has no database and no disk storage
Contact lists, address book, or social graphNone — never requested, never accessed
Analytics, advertising identifiers, or trackingNone — in the app or on this site
Payment identityNone — the subscription is processed by Apple or Google; we receive an anonymous entitlement, not your identity
Content scanningNone — sensitive-content checks (blurring) run on your device for your own display decisions and never leave it; nothing is blocked, logged, or reported

What passes through, transiently

To route your messages while you are connected, the relay necessarily handles, in memory only:

  • your IP address and connection times,
  • the sizes and timing of encrypted data,
  • an opaque routing identifier that reveals nothing about you.

This is held for the lifetime of your live connection, is never written to disk, and is never logged in production. The relay's source code is public, so this claim can be checked rather than believed.

Notifications, if you turn them on

Push notifications are off by default, per line. If you opt in, your device's push token is held by the person you paired with — inside your encrypted line, not on any server. When they knock while you're offline, the relay uses that token once to deliver a single fixed phrase — "New activity," with no sender, no channel, no content — and immediately discards it. The relay keeps no list of tokens. Apple's and Google's push services see only that same contentless phrase and the fact of its delivery.

This website

nimna.app sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from third parties. Our host may keep standard access logs for security and abuse prevention (IP address, requested page, time); we add nothing to that.

Requests from authorities

If we receive a lawful demand for user data, we can produce only what exists — and what exists is described above: nothing stored. There is no message archive, no account database, and no key escrow to hand over.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the change will be visible in this page's public history. Questions: hello@nimna.app.
