PROJECT: Nimna — a private line between two people.

WHAT IT IS: A paid, account-less, 1:1-only messaging app. Two people pair once
via a short code shared outside the app; from then on their channel is defined
by two device-held keypairs. Conversations are end-to-end encrypted and
synchronous-only — both must be online, like a phone call in text form. The
open-source relay stores nothing, ever: no accounts, no message queues, no
logs. Messages exist only on the two devices.

NAME MEANING: "Nimna" from Sanskrit/Hindi "nimn" (निम्न) — low, deep, minimal,
beneath notice. The brand story: everything kept low and minimal.

PRODUCT METAPHOR: A text call. A private line. A red phone between two people.
NOT a messenger replacement — a single-purpose object used for the
conversations that matter.

POSITIONING LINE: "A private line between two people. Nothing stored anywhere,
ever."

AUDIENCE: Discretion buyers (people who want specific conversations to exist
nowhere but two phones) and privacy-conscious professionals (lawyers,
journalists, dealmakers). Marketed with calm, adult confidence — never
spy-themed, never edgy, never winking at evasion.

BRAND PERSONALITY: Calm. Minimal. Quiet luxury. Discreet by design — the app
should look unremarkable on a home screen and feel premium inside. Think:
the design language of a beautiful safe or a fine analog object, not a
crypto/hacker aesthetic. Warmth over coldness; silence over noise.

TONE OF VOICE: Plain, honest, short sentences. States facts about the
architecture instead of making promises. Comfortable admitting limitations
(the threat-model honesty is a brand asset).

HARD AVOIDS: Neon green/matrix vibes, locks/shields/skulls/masks, spy or
detective imagery, fingerprints, binary-code textures, "military-grade"
language, fear-based marketing.

KEY PRODUCT RITUALS (design around these):
1. Generating & sharing a pairing code (incl. QR) — the "codeword" moment.
2. The safety phrase comparison after pairing.
3. The knock → accept → live session flow (the "call" ritual).
4. Composing while peer is offline → message waits locally → flows on connect.
5. Per-message disappearing timer set by the sender.
6. App lock (PIN/biometric) and the one-tap panic wipe.

TECHNICAL CONTEXT: Flutter app (iOS + Android). Protocol spec attached — read
it fully before designing; the architecture IS the brand.
