F8 — App settings (app lock · panic wipe · relay info · licenses)

The whole-phone controls. App lock is the zero-leak gate (USER decision). Panic wipe is hold-to-wipe, 2.5 s — a full second longer than a channel wipe — with voice string 8 verbatim. Relay info states plainly what the relay sees, and links the source.

F8.2 · App-lock setup
‹ App lock

Choose a PIN

When locked, the screen shows only the symbol and this pad. Nothing else — not even how many lines you have.

123 456 789 0⌫
Unlock with Face ID Recommended. Falls back to your PIN.

There's no way to recover a forgotten PIN — there's no account to prove you're you. Forgetting it means reinstalling, which wipes this phone's lines.

PurposeSet the zero-leak gate. The biometric toggle ships ON in this flow (spec §6: strongly recommended); the no-recovery consequence is stated at setup time, not discovered later.
Entry pointsF8.1 → App lock. The app also offers this setup once, right after the first channel is created (recommended-on posture; declinable).
Exit pointsPIN entered twice (confirm step, same frame pattern) → lock active → back to F8.1, caption now “On — PIN + Face ID”. ‹ → no lock, no scolding.
Edge casesPIN length: 6 digits shown; the pad accepts 4–8 (noted as a product decision in README, no protocol impact). Biometric hardware absent → toggle row hidden, PIN-only. Turning lock OFF later requires the current PIN first.

F8.3 · Panic wipe — hold 2.5 s
‹ Settings
App lock›
Notifications›

Wipe everything?

Keys and messages are destroyed on this phone. There is no backup, anywhere, by design — nothing can bring them back.

Wipe now

Hold for 2.5 seconds. Let go to keep everything.

[every line in the app burns down together while held; release at any point cancels — nothing happens. Longer than the 1.5 s channel wipe on purpose]

Keep
PurposeThe panic action: fast under stress, impossible by accident. Voice string 8 verbatim — including the button pair, where the safe word is “Keep”, warm, never “Cancel”.
Entry pointsF8.1 → Wipe everything.
Exit pointsHold completes (2.5 s) → all keys + all history destroyed atomically → F4.4 empty list, indistinguishable from a fresh install. Keep / release early / swipe down → F8.1, untouched.
Edge casesRelease cancels — nothing happens, ever partially. Destroying the keys makes any intercepted past ciphertext permanently undecryptable (spec §6 + forward secrecy, §7.3) — that's why wiping keys, not just messages, is the point. The subscription survives wipe and reinstall: it lives with the store identity (StoreKit / Play Billing), not this phone — F9.5 restores it.

F8.4 · Relay info
‹ Relay
Address wss://relay.nimna.app

The relay stores nothing. Verify it yourself.

Relay source code github.com/nimna/relay ›

While you're connected, the relay does see: your IP address, connection times, and the size and timing of what passes through. Never content, names, codes, or who you are.

[bring-your-own-relay is a planned pro feature (spec §8) — this screen grows an “Address” editor then, not before]

PurposeThe auditability claim, in the app itself: one address, one plain sentence, one link to the code, and the honest list of what IS visible (spec §4). Trust by inspection, not assertion.
Entry pointsF8.1 → Relay. Also linked from onboarding's F1.3 caption (“Details live in Settings → Relay”).
Exit pointsRelay source code → external browser (the one outbound link in the app). ‹ → F8.1.
Edge casesCopy here must never say “anonymous” — account-less is not anonymity, and the IP line keeps the claim honest (voice checklist). Relay unreachable → this screen still renders; connectivity states belong to the line in each chat, not to a settings page.
