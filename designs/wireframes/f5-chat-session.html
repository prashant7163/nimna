F5 — Chat session (offline → knock → live → media → hangup)

The session line under the header is never absent — its state IS the screen's truth. Dotted = not on the line, solid = knocking/live, always with a text label. New in v0.1.1: attachments — photos & files (spec §5.1), the sensitive-content whisper and blur (§5.3), the screenshot system line (§5.2), and the stale-knock hint (§6.1). New in v0.2.1: reactions & replies (spec §5.5, §5.8) — the long-press react/reply entry (F5.16), chips on text and media bubbles (F5.17), reply compose with the quoted preview (F5.18), and the tombstone EDGE (F5.19). Edge frames (dashed): sensitive whisper at attach · blurred received media · knock lapses · stale knock · line drops mid-session · reply tombstone. “Peer never accepts” is covered in F5.1/F5.13 annotations.
